scan-to-check local

Look up a cosmetic product, photograph its label or enter ingredients.

Decoding runs locally in your browser; camera frames are not uploaded to any service.

Recognition runs locally in your browser (Tesseract.js). The image is not uploaded. Review and correct the text before checking.

Product data from Open Food Facts / Open Beauty Facts (Open Database License 1.0). Label text was entered or recognized locally; no product database result is shown. Checks use the loaded EU snapshot, including CosIng. Product records are community supplied.
Regulatory information, not legal advice.
